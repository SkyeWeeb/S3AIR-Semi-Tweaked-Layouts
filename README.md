Semi-Tweaked Layouts is my personal take on how the levels of Sonic 3 & Knuckles could be made more fun. This mod is the culmination of 3+ years of me combing through the levels, finding tiny ways to make them flow better. While very similar to the vanilla game at first glance, the levels have been expanded and tweaked in both subtle and not-so subtle ways. Some acts contain Knuckles paths where there was nothing in the vanilla game. Slower areas have been made faster. Boring waiting sections have turned into fun platforming sections. Explore the mod for yourself!
